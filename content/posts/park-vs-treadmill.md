This morning I went to gym after a long time. Since it had been some time, I
decided to just walk on the treadmill for 30 minutes. By the half-time mark, my
mind started wishing for it to end soon! Not because I was tired but simply
because I was bored. In contrast, I have had numerous walks in 
[Victoria Park](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ctp4VYExUYkURtQx8) for over 50 minutes
without feeling the need for it to end.

Nothing helps make the treadmill walk pleasant — not music, not podcast,
nothing. It's purely mental grind. Whereas walk in the nature feels rewarding
in itself even if I take the same route every time.
